Safety should follow the citizen, not the other way around.

CGaaS separates the local conversation from the safety logic underneath it. That makes a trusted channel easier to protect, easier to teach through, and easier to extend.

Plug in the local channel. Keep the safety logic open.

The adapter changes by region. The core remains a clear sequence: normalize, route, verify, respond.

01Normalize the message+

LINE, WhatsApp, Viber, SMS, and USSD adapters translate local payloads into one standard event: user, message type, content, and region.

FRONTEND ADAPTER

02Route the intent+

The AI router identifies the user’s intent and selects the regional data source without coupling the core logic to a single app.

AI ROUTER

03Bring your own data+

A local police, health, or fact-checking endpoint can plug into the same contract. The source changes; the trust pattern stays recognizable.

BYOD DATA LAYER

04Respond with empathy+

The raw result becomes a respectful answer, followed by a localized micro-lesson when the moment can build confidence.

EMPATHY LAYER
